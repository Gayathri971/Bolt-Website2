Spec Driven Development Vs Harness Engineering

Spec Driven Development is the "what" layer — it defines intent, scope, and success criteria before execution starts, and it operates on a slow timescale (spec revised per feature or per gap found).

Harness engineering is the "how" layer — it defines execution mechanics, safety constraints, and error recovery, and it operates on a fast timescale (loop iterates per tool call).

Bad specs with a great harness get you a safely-executed wrong thing; a great spec with a weak harness gets you the right thing built chaotically or unsafely.
